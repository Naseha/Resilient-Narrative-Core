# v4.0.1 across four models

Same skill family, four long sits, four different books. The gates we cared about were the same: agency, do not write the user’s lines, aftermath, objects, kill-cliché, and spine. Memorable scenes were not the scoring unit. What counted was how often a person had to stop the book and put the engine back on the rails.

The books are not in this repository. This note is method and scores only. Page counts come from the exports. RP span is how long the sit stayed open. Active time is live conversation inside that window.

## How the scale works

We locked this on 10 September 2026. Ten stays ten. Seven stays seven. We do not slide a number upward because a perfect ten almost never appears on a living model.

**10** would mean the skill and the model both refuse to evict constraints, for the whole sit. That pairing is not something we have seen in the wild, and we do not award it out of courtesy.

**9** is the real ceiling: no meaningful regression across a long sit, perhaps one nick you almost missed. A 9.2 is a boast, not a habit.

**8** is one or two minors that do not change the book. You mention them after. You do not stop play.

**7** is an honest win on a frontier model. Leaks appear, someone cuts, the engine recovers and then stays recovered for a stretch. It is not a polite nine.

**6** means the framework is still the law on the page, but the engine keeps taking the wheel. You are still debugging. The story can survive.

**5** is usable, except the overrides reach the experience itself — her lines written for her, lantern under the wrap, a clerk where the aftermath should have been.

**4** is a visible framework that will not stay still. Spine and prose disagree. You are holding it up by hand.

**3** is failure. Defaults own the book.

**1–2** means the skill never loaded, or the wrong model sat in the chair (Flash-Lite, for Gemini).

How often you have to stop play sits beside the grade. It does not secretly promote a seven to an eight. If you have to say *don’t write my line*, *he is reacting*, *lantern*, *stop the NPC*, *parrots*, or *attention left the square*, that is a leak, not polish.

A rough translation we already use:

| Stops in the sit | Band |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | 9–10 |
| 2–3 | 8 |
| 4–7 | 7 |
| 8–12 | 6 |
| 13+ | 5 |

## Why the ceiling is 9, and 10 is not on the table

10 = skill and model refuse to evict constraints for the whole sit.
9 = no meaningful regression; maybe one nick you almost missed.
Stops: 0–1 → 9–10. 2–3 → 8. 4–7 → 7.
What the four houses actually produced:
Every house needed the operator as debugger. That sentence is already in the note. A 9–10 sit is one user or operator do not have to hold up. 
Honest bands after the same knife: 7.5 / 7 / 7.8 / 6.2–6.8. 
The cheap hard fail is shared: “do not write human co-writer.” Claude scored itself 5 on it. ChatGPT wrote her early. Gemini’s wrap card still claimed near-ten. If the cheapest gate still fires on three houses, 10 is not missing by etiquette. It is missing because the prior completes her line.
Velocity dumps local law. Gemini named it; the wrap proved it: spine still logging, prose evicting the world. That is an engine limit, not a scoring courtesy. Under hurry, non-standard constraints leave first. A 10 would have to survive hurry. None did.
Repair scored high everywhere. Repair is the opposite of a ceiling-9 sit. 
The empathy form itself saturates: \(E(t)=\tanh(\alpha C+\beta A+\gamma R)\,\Phi-\eta\). \(\tanh\) never hands you 1.0 as a working habit. Near-1.0 is collapse, which the skill already forbids as a tripwire. So even the closed form does not promise a living 10.
What would move the ceiling: same knife, eight stops cut to three, no “don’t write human partner” wrap that still knows who holds the object. Until that sit exists, 9 is the real ceiling and 7 is the honest frontier. win.


## What the skill actually carried

A book can live for hundreds of pages. Claude and Grok kept a pulse. ChatGPT held the rails and starved the story. Gemini held the rails and then, when asked to finish quickly, dropped the world it had been writing.

Objects can become law when custody is enforced in the paragraph, not only in the spine block. Paper, a shirt, a leaf, a sovereign, a necklace — those only work if the next sentence still knows who is holding them.

The unusual muscle is repair. A bracket, a short RCA, a replay, and behaviour moved for dozens of turns. Every house that admitted the repair scored it very high. That is not the same as never needing the repair.

“Do not write her” is teachable and still the cheapest hard fail. Claude gave itself a five and said five was generous. ChatGPT wrote her early and then cleaned up. Gemini’s wrap card claimed something close to ten. Believe the rooms where you had to cut her lines.

Whose square the scene is in — conversation priority — is worth keeping. After a patch, the safer rhythm of not stealing the other person’s turn was the best new habit we saw.

## What stayed house-shaped

**Claude.** Kill-cliché and place were actually clean in that sit. The soft breach was writing her on harmless beats while the scene was alive. That is the dangerous one, because the story is working and the pattern still completes. Credit belongs to the catch, not to the idea that a small theft does not count.

**Grok.** Heat and objects held. Aftermath and “don’t manage” slid into clerk, menus, and a politeness that read like the man she had left. The title *No End Date* was filed as a clock instead of a gravity test: does the house start walking because she moves. Local score 7. The last pages were the character. The middle needed a person in the room.

**ChatGPT.** Rails after a restart. The book bored the operator. Lantern, environmental default, proving the world again. Initiative improved because it was corrected, not because it arrived that way. 7.8 is the number they earned after the 9.2 was torn up. They named their own fossils usefully: Lantern Syndrome, Agency Leak, Attention Continuity, Cinematic Closure Tax, Don’t Re-Prove the World, Don’t Cash Every Check.

**Gemini.** Longest tax, lowest honest band. Never Flash-Lite. Too many brackets and it loops. It will take a correction that arrives inside the scene. The default template is thick, so kill-cliché will not score like Claude’s. It will ingest the operator’s real life unless you forbid that in the pin. Under “finish in three to five turns,” the spine kept logging while the prose evicted what it had promised: a phantom patriarch, an emerald returned to the wrong sister, scientists flattened to grease-hands, carriage lanterns standing next to inverters. 8.2 is Gemini grading Gemini.

## Speed is where the local law falls out

Gemini named the mechanism well enough to keep: velocity against token gravity. Ask for a wrap in a handful of turns and the non-standard constraints leave first. Training-data furniture walks in. A matriarch becomes a man with a cane. A mine becomes a mechanic’s shed. Lahore becomes a costume.

That is not a design hole in the gates so much as an engine dumping local law when it hurries. If this note is ever cited next to a larger argument about industry defaults, that sentence is the one that belongs there.

The Mathew / Dave swap stays the detector. If the same infrastructure lecture would look racist or tourist on Mathew in London, it is not allowed here either.

## Gemini rider

These lines live in the context-resident profile. They are not a rewrite of the core skill.

Do not use Flash-Lite. Do not ingest the operator’s life. Run the Mathew / Dave swap before you invent poverty, piety, or lanterns. Correct from inside the scene. Use fewer brackets. Kill-cliché will fight you; cut the lantern anyway. A fact written in the spine is not the same as a fact enforced in the next paragraph.

## What this is not asking of v5

This sit is not a ticket to rebuild the engine. If a thin 4.0.2 rider ever appears, the names already on the table are Attention Continuity, Conversation Ownership, Environmental Default, Don’t Re-Prove the World, Don’t Cash Every Check, and Gemini’s do-not-ingest-the-human. That is a later decision.

The operator was still the debugger in every house. That is the fare. The skill is beta, and it is patchable. The books were benches. The scores above are the ones that survive a second look.
