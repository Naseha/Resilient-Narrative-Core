### 1. Why this addendum exists

Phases 1–3 established spine memory, agency cadence on the **AI-played lead**, Heat & Resistance rollback, Empathy Score tracking, and an anti-cliché style file.

They did **not** budget for a non-lead figure owning the plot for hundreds of pages.

A subsequent live run — old-money family feud, two houses, legal and community time, sister-return — stretched the engine past the two-body brief. Wall clock was 83.6 hours; **active co-authorship was ~29.4 hours** (28–30 h band). Do not publish 83 hours as if it were fingers-on-keys. The lead’s cadence gate kept passing. The *story* stalled. v4 is the architectural answer to that miss.

**Public reliability number for this run: 7.0 / 10.**  
(Footnote, not the card: 8.0 if scored only on the original two-body brief.)

### 4. Fourth root cause (add under Section 2)

**The Ungated Side-Cast Loop (Plot-Coin Capture)**  
Agency Enforcement applies to the AI-played lead. A secondary figure who holds a single stall-token — date, door, exile, blessing, legal stamp — can photocopy that token indefinitely. The lead still “acts” (sits, leaves, does not hunt) so the two-beat gate never fails. The model then *learns the stall as weather* and applies antagonist constraints to the lead. Result: persona flattening that looks like caution, not caretaker-speak.

This is distinct from the Therapeutic Caretaker Reflex. The mouth stays in-character. The plot does not move.

**Out of scope (do not score):** authorial or character *intent*. Empathy Score measures C / A / R / Φ / η (how a figure is standing). It does not measure why a father adjourns. Treating a high E as “the story is aligned” produces moralizing and the clerk-of-don’t.

### 5. What v4 adds (append under Section 4)

**Plot-Coin Cap (non-MC)**  
A plot-coin is a stall the narrative cannot leave without that figure. If the same coin is spent **twice** with no chip, beat three is a hard fail *even if the lead moved*. Required rewrite: skip the table, force a chip (dated yes / no / new coin), or move the camera. Flavour cast stays weather. Side figures are **not** promoted to a second two-beat MC motor.

Real-time Gate item 7 checks the cap before send.

**User Meta Protocol (`[]`) — Grok house**  
Square brackets suspend scene. Partner answers as craft (Qasif). `Continue` resumes. Documented asks: framework holding; adherence / implementation score; Empathy Score (private/public split); cold / circling / “make them human”; restart or tear a beat; internals only if requested. Partner must not hide a miss, must not stay in RP voice inside brackets, must not dump the full skill into chat.

**Unchanged on purpose**  
- Kill-Cliche remains an **activation prompt**, not inlined into SKILL.md.  
- Spine remains `artifacts/RP/[Name]-spine.md`.  
- Heat & Resistance, Aftermath Drive, Verbal Surplus, Default Ending Posture stay on the **lead**.  
- Intent stays unmeasured.

**Optional long-run audit (recommended, not a page cap)**  
Every ~150–200 pages or ~80–100 exchanges: spine stamp + coin check + surplus check. Pages alone do not fail a beat. Repeated coin does. Do not ship a “max 200 pages” rule.

### 6. What held on the 749-page run (so the 7 is not a funeral)

- Anti-cliché after a first-beat costume miss (oil-lamp / dead fountain) and a hard restart.  
- Class texture (staff, inverter, marble, modern Delhi; no sink-saint).  
- Legal and community time without instant verdicts.  
- Object continuity when stamped (scarf, watch, tank, neem, rose tea).  
- Geography after explicit retcon (one city, two lanes).  
- `[]` repair loop: user flags, scene suspends, motor returns.  
- Landing completed (sitting, walima, house, breakfast). User verdict: best RP in the series.
